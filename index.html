◆ How it works

How it works

Three steps. Everything below is live at mint.

01 · MINT

First mint is free

One free mint per wallet (gas only). Paid mints after that are 0.001 BNB. Max 5 per wallet. Supply: 3,333.

02 · EVOLVE

Your agent grows

Hold your agent and it gains a ring every 7 days. The art visibly changes with age — the GIF evolves as you hold it.

03 · FORGE

Burn to upgrade

Burn 3 Commons to forge a Rare. Burn 2 Rares to forge an Epic. Burn 1 of each tier to forge a Mythic Forge King. Forging is the only way to get a Mythic.
